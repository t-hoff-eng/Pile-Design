Basis of design & limitations

  • Design geotechnical strength R*d,g = φg·Rd,ug ≥ E*d (AS 2159-2009 Cl. 4.3.1). φg = φgb + (φtf − φgb)K ≥ φgb, with φgb from the Average Risk Rating (Cl. 4.3.2) and K = 1.33p/(p+3.3) (static) or 1.13p/(p+3.3) (dynamic), K ≤ 1.
  • E*d = max(1.35G, 1.2G + 1.5Q); serviceability load G + ψsQ (AS/NZS 1170.0). Serviceability checked per AS 2159 Cl. 4.4.
  • Ultimate resistance: clay α·su and Nc·su; sand β-method (Kulhawy K/K0, δ/φ′) and Hansen/Vesic Nq·dq·σ′v; rock a·√qu (Rowe & Armitage 1987) and 4.83·qu0.51 (Zhang & Einstein 1998). Apply limiting values appropriate to the site, pile type and installation method.
  • Settlement: Randolph & Wroth (1978) elastic compressible-pile solution and Fleming (1992) hyperbolic method (both include elastic shortening).
  • Not included: structural strength (AS 2159 Section 5 / AS 3600 / AS 4100), negative skin friction / downdrag, group effects, lateral and uplift loading, cyclic loading, durability (AS 2159 Section 6) and pile self-weight. These must be checked separately.
  • This tool is a design aid. Results must be reviewed and verified by a qualified geotechnical engineer.
